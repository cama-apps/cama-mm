Ready Signal

Cama can alert you the instant a readycheck launches with a full lobby, or your shuffled match starts — as a dedicated push notification, a Discord DM, or both.

01

Two ways to get it

Same two triggers — a full-lobby readycheck, a shuffled match starting — delivered however you'd rather hear about them. Turn on one channel, the other, or both.

Push notification

ntfy.sh

Delivers to a dedicated app on your phone, completely independent of Discord — it still buzzes if Discord itself is muted, backgrounded, or signed out.

  • Needs the free ntfy app, installed once
  • Cama generates your topic; nothing to type or make up
Discord DM

A message from Cama

Arrives as a normal direct message, right inside Discord — nothing to install. It follows whatever notification settings you already have for Discord DMs.

  • Zero setup — flip a toggle and you're done
  • Handy if Discord already pushes DMs to your phone or desktop
02

Set it up

No admin involved — every player configures their own alerts from one panel.

1

Run /notify in Discord

Pick whichever channel(s) you want from the panel that comes up — both tracks below live in the same place.

Track A

ntfy.sh push notification

  1. Click Create ntfy Topic. Cama generates a long, private topic on the spot and shows it back to you — there's no field to fill in.
  2. Install the free ntfy app from the Play Store or F-Droid.
  3. In the app, subscribe to that exact topic, e.g. cama-8f2e91ab3c…. Keep it private — anyone who has it can publish to it.
Track B

Discord DM

  1. Click either (DM) toggle for the alert(s) you want.
  2. That's it — no app, no topic, nothing else to set up.

Whichever you pick, hit Send Test afterward to confirm delivery before you rely on it.

03

04

Good to know

Is this instead of Discord notifications?
No — it's in addition. Ready Signal exists because a normal Discord ping can get lost under mute settings, Do Not Disturb, or a phone that's asleep. This is a separate, dedicated channel just for these two moments.
Why a full lobby, not any readycheck?
A readycheck run against a half-empty lobby isn't the "everyone's here, go respond" moment worth a phone buzz. The alert only fires once the lobby has actually reached its ready threshold.
Do I need the ntfy app to use this?
No. The app is only for the ntfy push channel. Discord DM needs nothing installed — just toggle it on.
Can I use both channels at once?
Yes. Turn on, say, Match Started on both ntfy and DM and you'll get a push notification and a DM for the same event — useful if you're not sure which one you'll actually notice.
Can I turn off just one alert?
Yes, at the level of event and channel. Readycheck and Match Started toggle independently, and so do ntfy and DM for each — four switches total, any combination.
What if I don't need to react?
The readycheck alert only reaches players who still need to react — not someone who already confirmed or just joined.
Why can't I pick my own topic or server?
For safety, Cama generates your ntfy topic for you and only ever talks to the official ntfy.sh. A self-typed topic is easy to guess and a custom server is easy to abuse — taking both off the table closes that off.
Does an admin need to set anything up?
No. It's entirely self-service — each player configures their own alerts with /notify.
I want to stop entirely
Click Unsubscribe. Your ntfy topic and both DM toggles are cleared, and nothing more is sent until you run /notify again.
