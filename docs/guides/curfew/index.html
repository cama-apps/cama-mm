Curfew

Lock yourself out of the lobby on a schedule you set. A curfew window blocks you from queueing and quietly removes you from any lobby you're in — so "one more game" at 2am isn't a decision you have to keep making.

01

What a window actually does

Every window is a daily span of wall-clock time that you name yourself. While you're inside one, three things are true.

Blocked

You can't queue

Joining a lobby is refused — by command, by the Join button, and opening a new lobby too. You're told which window did it.

Removed

You're taken out

Already sitting in a lobby when the window starts? The bot removes you within a minute and DMs you to say why.

Private

Nobody else sees it

To the thread it looks like an ordinary leave. Your window's name, hours and timezone are never posted in channel.

02

Setting one up

Entirely self-service — no admin involved, and every reply is private to you.

/player curfew add name:sleep start:23:30 end:08:00

That's the whole thing. The remaining options are optional.

name requiredYours to choose — sleep, work, exams. Up to 40 characters. Reusing an existing name updates that window instead of making a second one.
start required24-hour HH:MM, e.g. 23:30. When the lockout begins.
end required24-hour HH:MM. When queueing unlocks again.
timezoneAn IANA name like Europe/Berlin. Leave blank and the window uses your /player timezone setting.
daysWhich nights it runs, e.g. Sa,Su or Mon,Wed,Fri. Blank means every day. Short forms M / T / W / Th / F / Sa / Su and full names both work.
modedefault or strict. See section 04 — strict is the one that stops you from talking yourself out of it.

You can have as many windows as you like. They're per-server and per-player, and they stack — if any one of them is active, you're locked.

03

Overnight windows pick the night, not the morning

A window whose end time is earlier than its start time runs past midnight into the next day. When you use days with one of those, the day you choose is the day it starts.

Fri
22:00 — locked
Sat
locked until 06:00
00:0006:0012:0018:0024:00

days:Fri on a 22:00 – 06:00 window covers Friday night through Saturday morning — not Saturday night. Pick the evening you want to stop playing, not the morning you want to wake up.

Heads up A window where start and end are the same time is treated as zero-length and never fires, rather than locking you out for a full 24 hours. If a window seems to do nothing, check that first.
04

Default or strict

Both enforce identically while active. They differ only in how easily you can back out of one.

default

Edit it whenever

Add, shrink, extend or delete a window at any moment, and the change is live immediately.

  • Good for a schedule you're still tuning
  • Nothing stops you lifting it at 1am
strict

Loosening waits for morning

Anything that frees up time — shrinking the window, switching it back to default, deleting it — doesn't take effect today. It's staged until 08:00 the next day, and the version committed this morning keeps enforcing tonight.

  • Extending a window still applies instantly
  • A brand-new strict window applies instantly too
  • The staged change lands on its own; you get a DM when it does
Why Strict mode exists to close the obvious bypass: deciding at 23:29 that tonight's curfew was a bad idea. Because a reduction is always staged to the next morning — even one made at 3am — whatever you committed as of this morning is what fires tonight.
05

Checking and clearing

/player curfew list Your curfew windows: - "sleep": 23:30 - 08:00 Europe/Berlin - "weekends": 02:00 - 10:00 Europe/Berlin starting Fri, Sa (runs into the next morning) [strict: edits/removal take effect the next morning]

/player curfew list shows every window you have, in its effective timezone, and flags any change that's staged and waiting. /player curfew remove name:sleep deletes one — instantly in default mode, next morning in strict.

06

Good to know

Which timezone does it use?
The window's own timezone if you set one, otherwise your /player timezone setting, otherwise America/New_York. Set /player timezone once and every window follows it.
What about daylight saving?
Handled. Windows are stored as wall-clock times in a named zone, so 23:30 stays 23:30 across a clock change instead of drifting by an hour.
My match already started — will it kick me?
No. Once you're locked into a forming match, the sweep leaves you alone. Curfew takes you out of a lobby, never out of a game that's already being made.
How fast is the removal?
The sweep runs about once a minute, so expect to be out within a minute of the window opening. Queueing is blocked the instant the window starts — that check is live, not swept.
Will people know I'm curfewed?
Not from the bot. The removal shows up in the thread as an ordinary leave, and the in-channel wording for a blocked join never names your window. Only you see the details, in a DM or a private reply.
Can I have different rules for weekdays and weekends?
Yes — that's what multiple named windows are for. A strict schoolnight on Mon–Thu and a looser weekend on Fri, Sa is a normal setup.
Does an admin have to do anything?
No. Curfew is entirely self-service, per player, per server.
I want out right now
/player curfew remove. In default mode you're free immediately. In strict mode you're not — that's the point of strict mode, and the reply will tell you when it lifts.
