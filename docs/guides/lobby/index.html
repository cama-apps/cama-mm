The Lobby Message

Every inhouse night starts with one pinned message in the matchmaking channel. It has two buttons that seat you and unseat you, and a row of reactions that each do something different — none of which is "join". This is what each one does.

01

What you're looking at

The bot posts one message per lobby and pins it. It opens a public thread named after the lobby, where joins, leaves and ready checks get logged.

🍽️ All You Can Feed
Open to all ratings Join to play! 📻 React for the live map and commentary on bot-hosted matches. Players selected for the match are excluded. Opened at 8:02 PM
Players (3) ⚔️Crystal · Tide · Rubick
⚔️ JoinLeave
⚔️jopacoin📋🔔📻

Two lobbies can be open at once: 🍽️ All You Can Feed, open to everyone, and 🧀 Whine & Cheese, limited to players below 1400 Glicko. You can sit in both.

/lobbyCreates the lobby if there isn't one, or shows you the existing one. Creating a lobby also seats you in it. The message and thread are the public announcement; the reply you get back is private and links you to the message.
/joinSame as the Join button, for people who prefer typing. Pick the lobby with the lobby option.
/leaveTakes you out of every lobby you're in. The Leave button only leaves the one lobby whose message you clicked.
02

Join and Leave

The buttons are the way in and out. A successful click is quiet: the only trace is a line in the lobby thread.

⚔️ Join

Seats you

You appear in the player list, the thread gets ✅ @you joined., and you're subscribed to the thread so you see what happens next. No ping, no public reply.

Leave

Unseats you

You come off the list and the thread gets 🚪 @you left. If a ready check is running, your confirmation is dropped with you.

Refused

Only you see why

Every refusal is an ephemeral reply — a message only you can see. Nothing about it is posted in the channel or the thread.

Reasons a join comes back refused, and what to do about each:

Not registeredRun /player register once. The bot needs a player record before it can seat you.
No roles setRun /player roles with your preferred positions, e.g. 123. The shuffle needs them.
Rating too high🧀 Whine & Cheese is for Glicko below 1400. You can still view it; you can't sit in it.
Lobby fullThe lobby has hit its player cap. Try the other lobby or wait for a leave.
Already inYou're already seated. Clicking Join again does nothing more.
CurfewOne of your curfew windows is active. The reply names it; nobody else sees that.
SuspendedAn admin has restricted you from this lobby. The reply gives the reason and term; /player lobby status has the details.
Pending matchYou're in a match that hasn't been recorded yet. Finish it with /record, then join.
Shuffle in progressYour current lobby is mid-shuffle or mid-draft. You can't hop lobbies until it resolves.
Slow downThree membership changes per 30 seconds, counting joins and leaves in both lobbies together. The reply says how long to wait.
03

What each reaction does

The bot pre-adds five reactions to every lobby message so they're one click away. Adding your own copy of a reaction triggers it. None of them joins the lobby — that's the button's job.

⚔️

Ready to play shout-out

Posts a line in the lobby thread saying you're up for a game. Think of it as raising your hand in the channel without joining yet — or as hype once you have.

⚔️ @you is ready to play a 5v5 Dota Challenge!

It does not seat you, and removing it does nothing. The sword used to be the join; the button replaced it because a reaction can't get a private reply, so refusals had to be posted publicly.

jopacoin

Here for the gamba spectator

For people who aren't playing but want in on the betting. Posts a line in the thread and subscribes you to it, so you see the shuffle and the odds without joining.

jopacoin @you is here for the gamba!

Ignored if you're already seated in that lobby.

📋

Lobby alert notification

Subscribes you to a ping for this lobby. When it gets within one or two players of a ready check, the bot posts 📢 Almost Ready! in the channel with a jump link, and mentions everyone who reacted 📋.

Want that for every lobby without reacting each time? /player lobby autonotify turns it on permanently. Players already in the lobby aren't pinged; they're the ones being waited on.

🔔

Ring a ready check same as /readycheck

Launches a ready check for that lobby: a fresh message in the thread listing everyone, with ✅ to confirm. You have to be in the lobby, and there has to be enough players. One difference from the command: the bell does not auto-confirm you, so click ✅ on the check too.

If it can't start — too few players, one just went out, you're not a member — the bot removes your bell so it doesn't look like something is pending. Full rules in How Ready Check Works.

📻

Radio spectator map

Subscribes you to the live map and commentary for bot-hosted matches that come out of this lobby. Remove the reaction to unsubscribe. Players picked for the match are excluded automatically, so you can't scout your own game.

The same reaction works on the shuffle message after teams are drawn.

Not on this message The ✅ reaction lives on the ready-check message in the thread, not on the lobby message. Adding it confirms you're ready; removing it withdraws. That's the one covered in How Ready Check Works.
Old habit If you've been reacting ⚔️ to join for years: it stopped seating people when the Join button arrived. Reacting now just posts the shout-out. Click Join.
04

Good to know

Why buttons instead of the reaction?
Discord lets a button answer you privately. A reaction can't — the only way to explain a refused reaction-join was to post it in the channel and delete it seconds later, which meant a curfew or suspension got announced to everyone.
Can I be in both lobbies?
Yes. Join each one's message. Whichever shuffles first takes you; you can't switch while that shuffle or draft is in progress.
Who gets pinged when I join?
Nobody, for an ordinary join. The thread line mentions you with the ping suppressed. When the lobby reaches the ready threshold, the bot posts 🎮 Ready! in the channel; one or two short of that, it posts 📢 Almost Ready! to the 📋 subscribers.
I left by accident
Click Join again. Just mind the limit of three changes per 30 seconds.
The bot removed me and I didn't click anything
Either a curfew window of yours started (you'll have a DM), a ready check swept you as unconfirmed, or an admin kicked you. The thread shows the leave; the reason, if it's private, comes to you directly.
What's the name shown next to me?
Your current server nickname, resolved live. Change it in Discord and the lobby follows.
